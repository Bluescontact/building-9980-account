## Correction note, September 28, 2026

The four PDFs are published as sent and are not edited. Three things in them are
corrected here.

1. **The dates of membership and years in the trade.** Attachment 1 gives his
   membership as "October 2017 – June 2, 2025," and Attachment 4 says he was a
   member "from October 2017" and "spent eight years in this trade." Both are
   wrong. He entered the elevator trade, and Local 131, on October 11, 2018 — about
   six and a half years before he left. Attachment 4's "two years as Recording
   Secretary" is correct.
2. **The withdrawal date.** The packet gives June 2, 2025 as his withdrawal date,
   as his later writing does; June 2 is also the day he handed the printed binder
   to the Business Representative in person. His resignation letter of May 21,
   2025 already included his Local 131 membership, and the Local replied in
   writing on June 3, 2025 that it could not find his withdrawal letter.
3. **The display name in the email headers.** The headers reproduced in
   Attachment 2, Part A show the Local's address with a display name. That name
   belongs to the office account: it is the name of the Business Representative
   before the current one, left on the account because the address stayed with
   the office. Every email from the Local's address was written by the current
   Business Representative, Marvin Regensberg. The PDF carries the display name
   as sent; this repository does not repeat it.
