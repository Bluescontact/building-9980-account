# Gift — the decon procedure

**Who.** Every hoistway mechanic who opens a pit or a car top in hantavirus country —
and, as the receiving seats, the training apparatus of every Local: the Safety
Representative who can mark it, the industrial hygienist who can fill its blanks, the
apprenticeship instructor who can teach its one hour. This is the hazard half of the
Sep 5, 2026 letter to the Business Agents of 74 Locals; the conduct half of that letter
belongs to the ledger, not to this file.

**What is given.** [The one-page minor-decontamination procedure]
(documents/Hoistway_Rodent_Decon_Procedure.md) — recognition, the
spray-and-wait / stop-and-call split, the wet method, PPE order, and policy hooks —
given deliberately unfinished, twice over:

1. The **[IH] thresholds are blank** because setting them is an industrial hygienist's
   authority, not a mechanic's. A procedure that arrived complete would be asserting a
   competence the giver does not hold; the blanks are where the receiver's own
   qualified mark goes. The gift is the frame, not the numbers.
2. The **policy hooks are blank** because who calls the reopen, who pays for the wait,
   and which article carries the training duty are each Local's own agreement to cite.
   Filling them is the act by which a Local makes the procedure its own.

The license is on its face: *"Tear it up and write a better one."* A Local that
supersedes this draft with its own IH-marked version has used the gift completely.

**What it serves, in the receiver's terms.** A hazard every Local has and no
apprenticeship curriculum covers: this record's originating event began with droppings
on equipment a mechanic was expected to disturb dry, on a site where the training and
PPE duty existed on paper (NEBA Art. XXVII Par. 4) and was never executed. One page,
one classroom hour, closes that gap for a Local at near-zero cost — and the Local, not
the giver, becomes the author of the version that counts.

**The question held.** None. This gift travels without one — it was given
unconditionally, and the only question the Sep 5 letter asked (each officer's judgment
under Art. XVIII §2) belongs to a different instrument and a different file. A gift
that carried a question about the conduct arc would make the hazard hostage to the
dispute; the two are kept apart here for the same reason the gifts and charges arcs
are kept apart in the [README](README.md).

**What closes the entry.** Adoption, countable: a named Local (or site, or JATC)
adopting a named version — this draft IH-marked, or a successor it provoked — as a
handout, a toolbox talk, or an apprenticeship hour. This is the same closure form the
[next-worker gift](next-worker.md) names as the arc's only success condition no seat
controls; this procedure is that gift's first concrete instance. Supersession closes it
as completely as adoption does.

**The falsifier.** An existing decon SOP, toolbox talk, or apprenticeship module that
already covers rodent contamination in hoistways for elevator mechanics — at any Local,
any elevator company, or the NEIEP curriculum. If it exists, this gift is redundant
where it exists, which is the best possible outcome, and this file will say so the day
it is identified.

**State.** Built Sep 5, 2026; routed the same day to the published BA addresses of 74
of 75 Locals (RECORD entry 22 — one Local publishes no address; Local 3 re-sent to its
current address same day). One address-change auto-notice and one out-of-office
auto-reply received; no substantive reply; the seats' silence dated from Sep 5, 2026.
Deposited in this repository Sep 11, 2026. No industrial-hygiene review; carries no
authority until a Safety Representative and an industrial hygienist have marked it.
