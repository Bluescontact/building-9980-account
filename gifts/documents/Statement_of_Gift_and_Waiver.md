# **Statement of Gift and Waiver**

**Date:** May 21, 2025  
 **From:** Kevin Mears  
 **To:** Kone Corporation and IUEC Local \#131

## **Purpose Statement**

I, Kevin Mears, am providing this statement to clearly establish my intentions regarding the documentation framework I've developed and am now offering to Kone. This statement clarifies the nature of my gift, the specific rights I'm granting, and the rights I'm retaining as the framework's creator.

## **The Gift I'm Offering**

I am freely offering the following materials to Kone without expectation of compensation:

1. The Worker Safety Documentation Template  
2. The Alignment Revelation Framework  
3. The Economic Analysis of Safety Documentation  
4. All implementation examples from the Sandia project

This offering reflects my personal commitment to improving safety practices within our industry. I believe this framework has the potential to create substantial value by aligning worker protection, operational continuity, and regulatory compliance.

## **Grant of Full Usage Rights**

I hereby grant to Kone and IUEC Local \#131 full and unrestricted rights to use, implement, and adapt the documentation framework I've developed, including:

* The right to use the template and framework in any manner deemed appropriate  
* The right to modify or adapt the materials to suit organizational needs  
* The right to incorporate the approach into existing safety management systems  
* The right to share the framework with contractors, subcontractors, and clients  
* The right to develop training materials based on these concepts  
* The right to create derivative works based on the original framework

This grant includes a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, non-exclusive license to use the framework and methodology in any manner Kone or the union deems appropriate, with a single condition \- that the explicit reference to OSHA Section 11(c) protection remain in all future adaptations of the template.

## **Retained Rights**

While granting the above usage rights, I retain the following:

1. **Independent Development Rights** \- I retain the right to continue developing, refining, and implementing this framework independently, outside the scope of my employment contract with Kone or membership with IUEC Local \#131.  
2. **Rights to Future Versions** \- I retain all rights to any future versions, adaptations, or enhancements I may develop independently after my resignation.

These retained rights in no way limit the usage rights granted to Kone and the union. Both parties have full permission to use and adapt the current framework regardless of any future independent development I may undertake.

## **Waiver of Liability Claims**

I explicitly waive any and all potential claims, demands, actions, or causes of action that I may have against Kone, its officers, directors, employees, and affiliates, arising from:

* Lost wages due to safety-related work stoppages during my employment  
* Administrative delays resulting from my safety reporting and documentation  
* Any economic impacts I experienced while exercising my right to report safety concerns

This waiver specifically includes claims related to the documented incidents on the Sandia project that resulted in work stoppages and administrative delays.

## **My Personal Motivation**

I'm offering this framework because I believe it embodies a better way to approach safety documentation \- one that serves all stakeholders simultaneously. Throughout my career, I've seen how fragmented approaches to safety often create unnecessary conflict between worker protection and project completion. The framework I've developed addresses this problem directly by revealing the natural alignment between safety and productivity.

This framework represents my best understanding of how documentation can transform organizational dynamics around safety, and I wish to see it implemented broadly, regardless of my continued employment.

## **Period of Silence**

I commit to a three-month period of silence regarding this framework and the Sandia project, from May 22nd to August 22nd, 2025\. During this period, I will not initiate contact with Kone, the union, or the DOE regarding these matters.

This silence allows Kone to evaluate the framework independently without any pressure or influence from me. I believe the value of this approach will become evident through its implementation rather than through my continued advocacy.

## **Future Possibilities**

After the silence period concludes, I remain open to discussion about implementation on other DOE projects, should that serve mutual interests. If Kone identifies value in my continued involvement, I would be open to discussing a consulting relationship focused specifically on implementation.

This openness comes without expectation or requirement \- simply an acknowledgment that future collaboration remains possible if Kone finds it valuable.

## **Conclusion**

This statement and waiver are made freely and voluntarily, with full understanding of their implications. My intent is to provide Kone and IUEC Local \#131 with complete freedom to implement this framework while preserving my rights as its creator to continue developing these concepts independently.

I believe this approach has the potential to create substantial value for all parties involved. The framework represents my personal contribution to advancing safety practices in our industry \- offered in appreciation for the professional development I've gained during my time with the company and union.

Sincerely and with clear intent,

Kevin Mears
