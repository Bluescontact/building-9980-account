# The documents themselves

These are the documents given to Kone Inc. and IUEC Local 131, reproduced as sent. The
first four are the May 2025 gift packet — the bound packet delivered to Local 131
leadership on May 14, 2025, and the resignation-and-gift set of May 21, 2025. The two
guides are later Recognition Infrastructure documents (January 2026) developed from the
same work, addressed to the two seats the packet went to: the business agent's and the
field manager's.

**The May 2025 packet:**

- [Formal Resignation and Framework Gift](Formal_Resignation_and_Framework_Gift.md) —
  May 21, 2025, to Kone Management and IUEC Local #131; names the packet's contents
- [Statement of Gift and Waiver](Statement_of_Gift_and_Waiver.md) — May 21, 2025, to
  Kone Corporation and IUEC Local #131; the waiver of every wage and delay claim, and
  the grant of full usage rights with one condition (the OSHA §11(c) reference stays)
- [The Worker Safety Documentation Template](Safety_Documentation_Template.md) — the
  template itself
- [The Alignment Revelation Framework](Alignment_Revelation_Framework.md) — the
  meta-template behind it

**The guides (January 2026):**

- [Business Agent's Guide to Recognition Infrastructure](Business_Agent_Recognition_Infrastructure_Guide.md)
  — for the union seat
- [Field Manager's Guide to Union Relations](Field_Manager_Union_Relations_Guide.md)
  — for the company seat

Nothing here asks for anything. The waiver came first; these are the gifts it cleared
the ground for. The per-stakeholder development of what each gift serves is one level
up, in [gifts/](../README.md).
