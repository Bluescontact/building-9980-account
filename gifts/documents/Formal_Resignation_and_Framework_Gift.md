# **Formal Resignation and Framework Gift**

**Date:** May 21, 2025  
 **From:** Kevin Mears  
 **To:** Kone Management and IUEC Local \#131

## **Formal Notice of Resignation**

I hereby resign from my position as Journeyman Elevator Mechanic with Kone, effective Wednesday, May 21st, 2025\. This resignation also includes my membership in IUEC Local \#131. My final day will be dedicated to completing essential tasks and ensuring a smooth transition for the next mechanic.

## **The Gift I've Created**

During my time on the Sandia project, I've developed a structured approach to safety documentation that transforms how organizations handle safety concerns. I'm offering this approach to Kone as a gift, freely given without expectation of compensation or recognition.

This documentation framework emerged from a personal realization: that when properly documented, safety concerns can simultaneously serve worker protection, operational continuity, and regulatory compliance. I've tested this approach on the Sandia project and seen firsthand how it creates value for all stakeholders.

The enclosed package includes:

1. The **Worker Safety Documentation Template** \- A practical tool that transforms safety reporting  
2. The **Alignment Revelation Framework** \- The core principles that give the template its effectiveness  
3. The **Economic Analysis** \- Concrete evidence of how this approach creates measurable value  
4. The **Statement of Gift and Waiver** \- Formal clarification of how I'm offering this to Kone

## **Why This Matters**

The framework I'm providing isn't just a documentation system. It's a direct expression of my personal values around integrity, clear communication, and finding harmony between safety and productivity. I've seen firsthand how this approach:

* Creates protection for workers who report legitimate safety concerns  
* Maintains project momentum by identifying alternative productive work  
* Establishes clear documentation for appropriate cost recovery  
* Transforms potentially adversarial situations into opportunities for resolution

These results align perfectly with what I care about most professionally \- creating environments where people can work both safely and effectively.

## **No Strings Attached (Almost)**

I offer this framework freely, with just one condition \- that the explicit reference to OSHA Section 11(c) protection remain in all future adaptations of the template. This reference provides essential legal protection for workers who document safety concerns in good faith.

Beyond this single condition, Kone is free to use, modify, and implement this framework in whatever way serves the organization best.

## **Next Steps**

From May 22nd to August 22nd, 2025, I will maintain a period of silence regarding this framework and the Sandia project. This creates space for Kone to evaluate the approach independently, without any pressure or influence from me.

After this period, I'm open to discussing implementation on other DOE projects if that would be valuable. This isn't an expectation \- just an open door if Kone finds value in further conversation.

## **My Path Forward**

My resignation marks a personal transition to work that more fully aligns with my values. I'll be partnering with my father to build aquaponic systems in New Mexico \- work that combines technical skill with sustainable food production.

I leave Kone with appreciation for the experiences and knowledge I've gained here. The challenges we've faced on the Sandia project have shaped my understanding of how safety, documentation, and operational effectiveness can align when properly approached.

With sincere respect,

Kevin Mears
